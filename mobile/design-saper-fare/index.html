Design & Saper Fare / 01

Le Proporzioni

The Beauty of Balance

The character of a boat begins in its proportions: the relationship between length and height, the tension of a surface, the position of a cockpit, the moment a sheerline meets the stern. These decisions shape how a design is recognised long before its details are noticed. The ambition for VELLARIO is to develop an original language that is expressive without being fashionable.

0205

Design & Saper Fare / 02

Il Segno

Distinctive at First Sight

A memorable exterior should be unmistakable from a distance and rewarding up close. The model studies explore different characters rather than a single formula applied at different sizes. As the range develops, our task is to identify the proportions, graphic signatures and architectural principles that can connect them as a family without compromising what makes each one distinctive.

0305

Design & Saper Fare / 03

La Materia

Considered in Every Detail

La materia tells part of the story of every important object. The feeling of a handrail, the meeting of two surfaces, the finish around a control: small gestures of design that deserve the greatest care. VELLARIO's intended material language is rooted in authenticity, restraint and the Italian idea of saper fare.

0405

Design & Saper Fare / 04

Bellezza e Ingegneria

A boat is architecture in motion. The beauty of its proportions must coexist with the demands of balance, structure, ergonomics and the sea. For this reason, design and naval engineering cannot be separate conversations. As the collection develops, technical expertise must strengthen the character of every project rather than dilute it.

0505

Design & Saper Fare / 05

Su Misura

An Expression of You

Su Misura means making a design personal without making it lose itself. We envisage a considered approach to colours, finishes and details that respects the character of each boat. Every future choice must be as technically credible as it is aesthetically distinctive.
