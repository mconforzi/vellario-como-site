Contatti

01

Il Piacere di Conoscersi

A relationship with VELLARIO is intended to begin with an appreciation of design and a shared understanding of what makes a boat personal. The design house is taking shape around a carefully selected collection and an individual approach to future clients. Its public story begins here, with the vision and the work behind it.

02

Un Contatto Personale

A dedicated channel for personal introductions will be announced as the design house develops. Until then, the collection and the story of VELLARIO can be explored here without registration or the sharing of personal information.

03

Riservatezza

A relationship built on discretion deserves a correspondingly careful approach to information. Before enquiries are accepted, VELLARIO intends to establish a clear process for contact requests, personal data and responses. No contact information is collected on this preview site.
