Contact

Every extraordinary object begins with an idea. Every lasting relationship begins with a conversation.

01

The Right Conversation

A relationship with VELLARIO is intended to begin with an appreciation of design and a shared understanding of what makes a boat personal. The maison is taking shape around a carefully selected collection and an individual approach to future clients. Its public story begins here, with the vision and the work behind it.

02

Introductions Will Open Here

A dedicated channel for personal introductions will be announced as the maison develops. Until then, the collection and the story of VELLARIO can be explored here without registration or the sharing of personal information.

03

Considered from the First Contact

A relationship built on discretion deserves a correspondingly careful approach to information. Before enquiries are accepted, VELLARIO intends to establish a clear process for contact requests, personal data and responses. No contact information is collected on this preview site.
