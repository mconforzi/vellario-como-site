04 / The Larian Tradition

History & Legacy

The history belongs to the lake. Our task is to understand it before proposing a new chapter.

01

A Culture of Water

Boats on Lake Como have served work, transport, sport and leisure. The Museo Barca Lariana documents these traditions through historic vessels and objects.

Source: Museo Barca Lariana · Collection

02

From Workboats to Runabouts

The museum's collections include rowing and working boats, sailing craft, leisure motorboats and racing hulls—a history wider than the familiar polished runabout.

Source: Museo Barca Lariana · Museum

03

Craft & Competition

Historic makers and performance craft form part of the Larian tradition. Source material must distinguish the work of established builders from the future VELLARIO brand.

Source: Museo Barca Lariana · Heritage

04

Preserving the Memory

The museum at Pianello del Lario occupies a former nineteenth-century silk mill and preserves the material culture of boating on the lake. No relationship with VELLARIO is claimed.

Source: Regione Lombardia · Museo Barca Lariana

05

A Contemporary Interpretation

VELLARIO proposes a design-led interpretation of these cultural references. This is inspiration, not a claim of inherited shipyard history. Riva was founded at Sarnico on Lake Iseo, not on Lake Como.

Source: Riva · Official History
