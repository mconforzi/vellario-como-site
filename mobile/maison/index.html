06 / The Maison · An Italian Point of View

The Maison

A new name with a clear ambition: to make nautical design a deeply personal expression of Italian elegance.

01

A Maison Begins with a Conviction

VELLARIO begins with a conviction: a boat can belong among the most distinctive design objects, admired for its identity, precision and individuality as much as for the experience it offers. The ambition is not a catalogue without limits. It is a maison with a recognisable point of view, a considered collection and the discipline to protect both.

02

Como as Inspiration. Italy as Culture.

The Lario gives VELLARIO its emotional and visual starting point. The wider culture is Italian: an appreciation of design that balances beauty with use, craft with invention and individuality with restraint. The intention is to carry these values into a contemporary nautical language without imitating the boats—or claiming the accomplishments—of those who came before.

03

The Brand at the Centre

The proposed VELLARIO model places design and identity at the centre. The maison intends to guide styling, product development, the selection of materials, quality expectations and the relationship with each future client. Specialised construction is envisaged through carefully selected partner shipyards. This structure allows the brand to concentrate its attention where it matters most: on the character of each boat and the experience around it.

04

A More Personal Way to Begin

An important choice deserves time, understanding and a direct exchange. We envisage a journey in which design is introduced personally, preferences are explored with discretion and every decision belongs to a coherent vision. Rather than presenting an endless array of options, the aim is to offer thoughtful individual expression within a distinctive VELLARIO identity.

05

A Future Built Deliberately

A new maison takes shape one decision at a time: defining its identity, refining a collection, bringing design and technical disciplines together, and choosing the people able to realise its ambition. VELLARIO's future will be measured less by the number of boats it introduces than by the distinctiveness and integrity of each one.
