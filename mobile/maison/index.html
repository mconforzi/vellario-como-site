06 / The Maison · An Italian Point of View

The Maison

A new name with a clear ambition: to make nautical design a deeply personal expression of Italian elegance.

01

A Maison Begins with a Conviction

VELLARIO begins as an idea before it becomes a fleet: that a boat can belong in the world of the most distinctive design objects, admired for its identity, thoughtfulness and individuality as much as for the experience it offers. The ambition is not to build a large range for everyone. It is to create a maison with a recognisable point of view and the discipline to protect it.

02

Como as Inspiration. Italy as Culture.

The Lario gives VELLARIO its emotional and visual starting point. The wider culture is Italian: an appreciation of design that balances beauty with use, craft with invention and individuality with restraint. The intention is to carry these values into a contemporary nautical language without imitating the boats—or claiming the accomplishments—of those who came before.

03

The Brand at the Centre

The intended business model is deliberately focused. VELLARIO aims to direct its identity, styling, product development, specifications, quality expectations and relationships with future clients. Construction is envisaged through selected external specialist shipyards, subject to agreements and verification. This approach is intended to concentrate attention on the distinctiveness of each project without the burden of unnecessary industrial infrastructure.

04

A More Personal Way to Begin

The relationship should begin with a conversation, not a checkout. We envisage clear presentation of the collection, discreet discussion of individual preferences and a direct exchange between the future owner and those guiding the brand. Details of client access, personalisation, purchasing and after-sales support will be announced only when the service is genuinely ready to be delivered.

05

A Future Built Deliberately

VELLARIO is at the stage where identity, design language, source projects and the future collection are being defined. The next decisions concern product strategy, technical feasibility, selected production relationships and a coherent way to introduce the brand to the world. We would rather make these decisions carefully than create the appearance of a history that has not yet happened.
