The Collection

Five original design studies. One ambition: to bring a distinctive Italian point of view to the water.

01

Distinct by Nature

Some boats express their character through pure lines, others through movement, versatility or presence. The VELLARIO collection brings together five different projects from the original design archive. They are presented individually because each deserves a voice of its own. Explore the concepts through their original imagery and the design ideas that shaped them.

02

Design Before Declaration

The collection currently brings together archival concepts being considered as a foundation for the future VELLARIO range. Renderings and films communicate design intent; they are not an announcement of production, availability or approved specifications. The next stage will establish which elements belong to each definitive model and how the maison's shared design language unites them.

03

An Individual Expression

A remarkable boat begins with a recognisable identity, but its most personal details should respond to the person who will enjoy it. The intended VELLARIO approach places colours, finishes, textures and considered individual choices within a coherent overall design. The scope of future personalisation will be defined alongside each project's technical development and production planning.

04

The Beginning of a Relationship

The future commercial experience is intended to be direct, considered and personal, rather than built around volume or an impersonal configurator. Dedicated client introductions and project information will be made available when the relevant operational arrangements have been verified.
