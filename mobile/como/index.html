01 / Como · The Origin of the Vision

Como

A singular meeting of water, architecture and landscape. The inspiration behind VELLARIO.

01

The Character of the Lario

At Lake Como, the mountains do not frame the water from a distance; they rise directly around it. Towns follow the shoreline, stone steps reach small landing stages, and the view changes with every bend of the lake. Here, the journey matters as much as the destination. It is this dialogue between movement and place that informs the atmosphere we want VELLARIO to express.

Source: Regione Lombardia · Lake Como

02

Architecture in Conversation with Water

The great villas of Como were designed to be experienced within their setting, not separated from it. At Villa del Balbianello, on the wooded Lavedo peninsula, architecture, terraces and gardens form an extraordinary composition above the lake. Its history belongs to those who created and preserved it; its sense of proportion offers a lasting lesson in elegance.

Source: FAI · Villa del Balbianello

03

The Art of Arriving

On the lake, the perspective is reversed: a villa reveals itself from the water, a village appears beyond a headland, and an ordinary crossing becomes a moment of anticipation. This is the kind of experience at the heart of the VELLARIO vision: unhurried, intimate and inseparable from its surroundings. Not luxury added to a journey, but beauty found within it.

04

A Living Nautical Culture

Como's relationship with boats reaches far beyond leisure. Workboats, sailing craft, passenger vessels, racing machines and classic motorboats have all left their mark on its shores. The Museo Barca Lariana at Pianello del Lario preserves a remarkable record of this culture, making the lake's boating history tangible through vessels, engines, documents and stories.

Source: Museo Barca Lariana

05

A New Interpretation

VELLARIO does not claim to continue a historic shipyard or possess a century-old archive of its own. It takes something different from Como: a cultivated understanding of beauty, an affection for boats and the conviction that contemporary design can feel entirely at home in a timeless setting. The connection is one of inspiration and intent.
