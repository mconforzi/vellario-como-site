01 / The Place

Como

The landscape behind the vision. Water, stone, light and a culture shaped by the lake.

01

The Lake

The Lario is not a decorative background to the brand. Its relationship between mountains, shorelines and water is the geographic starting point for VELLARIO.

Source: Regione Lombardia · Lake Como

02

Villas & Architecture

The lake's villas are part of an enduring architectural culture. Villa del Balbianello, on the promontory of Lavedo in Lenno, is a documented example of its layered history.

Source: FAI · Villa del Balbianello

03

Living With Water

At Como, moving across the water changes the perspective of place. The shore is experienced through approach, distance, arrival and return. This is a creative direction, not a historical claim.

04

Nautical Culture

The Museo Barca Lariana at Pianello del Lario preserves historic boats, motors and nautical records, providing a documented foundation for understanding the lake's boating culture.

Source: Museo Barca Lariana

05

The Inspiration

VELLARIO draws creative inspiration from this context. The intention is to develop a contemporary nautical identity connected to the Lario without claiming the achievements of historical boatyards as its own.
