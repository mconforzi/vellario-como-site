La Collezione / 01

Ogni Barca, un Carattere

Five Distinct Characters

Some boats express their character through pure lines, others through movement, versatility or presence. The VELLARIO collection brings together five different projects from the original design archive. They are presented individually because each deserves a voice of its own. Explore the concepts through their original imagery and the design ideas that shaped them.

La Collezione / 02

La Forma del Desiderio

Every VELLARIO design begins with an idea of character. These five studies come from an original nautical design archive, where each silhouette and arrangement was developed around its own intentions. The images offer a first encounter with these ideas; the design house's next chapter is to refine their distinct personalities into a coherent collection.

0304

La Collezione / 03

Su Misura

The Art of Making It Personal

Su Misura is not the promise of unlimited choice. It is the ability to understand an individual taste and translate it into thoughtful details without losing the character of the design. The scope of VELLARIO personalisation will be established through future product development.

0404

La Collezione / 04

Un Invito Personale

A memorable experience begins long before a boat enters the water. The VELLARIO approach to the future owner relationship is intended to be personal, discreet and informed by design. Discover the collection, understand the characters and follow the design house as that relationship takes shape.
